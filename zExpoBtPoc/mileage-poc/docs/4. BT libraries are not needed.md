---
### 🚫 Why BLE libraries are unnecessary

Your BLE POC already proved the key point:
- Your cars do not advertise BLE
- BLE scanning cannot detect them
- BLE cannot detect connection state
- BLE cannot differentiate vehicles

So BLE is out.

---
### 🚫 Why Classic Bluetooth libraries are unnecessary
Codex was correct about this part:
- iOS blocks Classic Bluetooth scanning
- iOS hides MAC addresses
- iOS does not expose Classic BT connection state
- No RN library can bypass this
- Even native Swift apps cannot scan for Classic BT devices

So Classic Bluetooth is out too.

---
### ⭐ The Real Solution (and the one that works on every iPhone)

You only need one thing:
#### 🎧 Audio Route Detection via AVAudioSession

This gives you:
- When the phone connects to a car
- When the phone disconnects
- The name of the connected car audio device
- The audio profile (A2DP, HFP, CarAudio, etc.)
- A stable identifier per vehicle

This is the exact mechanism used by:
- MileIQ
- Everlance
- TripLog
- Driversnote
- SherpaShare
They all rely on audio route changes, not Bluetooth scanning.
---
### 🚗 Why audio route name solves your “which car?” problem

When your phone connects to a car, the audio route name is something like:
- “TOYOTA CAMRY”
- “HIGHLANDER”
- “FORD SYNC”
- “Uconnect”
- “MyMazda”
- “CAR MULTIMEDIA”

This name is:
- Stable
- Unique per vehicle
- Exposed by iOS
- Accessible from React Native
- Works with or without CarPlay
- Works with or without BLE
- Works with or without Classic BT scanning

This is your vehicleKey.

---
### 🧠 What your app actually needs

Your app only needs to know:

1. Am I connected to a car?

→ Audio route = CarAudio / BluetoothA2DP / BluetoothHFP

2. Which car is it?

→ audioRouteName

3. Is the car on or off?

→ Audio route connect/disconnect

→ CarPlay connect/disconnect (optional)

4. Is the phone moving?

→ Location speed &gt; threshold

That’s it.

No Bluetooth libraries required.

#### ✔ You do NOT need BLE libraries
#### ✔ You do NOT need Classic Bluetooth libraries
#### ✔ You ONLY need audio route detection + movement detection

This is the cleanest, most reliable, most iOS‑friendly architecture.
